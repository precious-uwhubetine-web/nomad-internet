Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

  • Will Nomad work while driving and parked?

    Yes! Nomad is designed specifically for truck drivers. It stays connected while you're driving on the highway and when you're parked at rest areas, truck stops, or loading docks. The modem automatically finds and connects to the strongest available network, so you stay online wherever the road takes you.

  • Do I have to install anything in my truck?

    No. Just plug the modem into power, turn it on, and connect your devices. There is no drilling, mounting, or professional install required.

  • Is there really no data limit or throttling?

    Correct. You can stream, video call, and use the internet as much as you want without hitting data caps or slowdowns.

  • What if it doesn't work where I travel?

    You are never locked in. You can cancel anytime, and the annual plan includes full modem protection for extra peace of mind.

  • Can I use this for multiple devices in my cab?

    Yes. Phones, tablets, laptops, and TVs can all connect to the same Nomad network at the same time.
