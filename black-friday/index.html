FAQ

  • What speeds should I expect?

    Residential plans typically support speeds up to 100 Mbps, depending on your location and network conditions. Travel plans typically support speeds up to 200 Mbps. Actual performance varies by area, signal strength, and nearby network activity.

  • Does weather affect performance?

    Yes. Severe weather such as storms, heavy rain, or dense cloud cover may temporarily affect signal strength. Most customers remain connected, but speeds can vary during harsh weather.

  • Do I rent the modem or own it?

    Your modem is provided as a rental at no additional cost for the duration of your subscription. You don't purchase the device. If you cancel your plan, you'll be required to return the modem following the return instructions provided.

  • Do I need to sign a contract?

    No. Nomad plans do not require service contracts, and you can cancel at any time. Multi-month plans remain active for their full term, and are non-refundable beyond the initial 14-day cancellation window.

  • Do plans work everywhere?

    Generally speaking, yes. Nomad provides nationwide coverage options, however, performance may vary based on proximity to cellular towers. If you have a question about coverage in your area, please contact our support team by phone at 1-281-800-1000, email at support@nomadinternet.com, or chat with us directly from the website.

  • Can I pause my service?

    Yes. All travel plans include this functionality as a standard feature. The travel upgrade option is an available add-on for all residential plan subscribers.
